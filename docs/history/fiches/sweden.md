| Date de référence | 1911 (peacetime permanent cadres) |
| Définition | Permanent cadres about 22,000 officers, NCOs and specialists — the standing peacetime establishment around which the conscript army was trained. In addition c.85,000 men were trained annually as recruits or recalled for further training, so the number under arms at any one time fluctuated. Conscripts served 240 days (infantry, position/fortress artillery, fortress engineers, army service corps) or 365 days (cavalry, field artillery, field engineers, field telegraph) under the 1901 law. |

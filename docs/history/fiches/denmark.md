### B. Réserves mobilisables

| Champ | Contenu |
|---|---|
| Valeur retenue | **80 000** |
| Date de référence | 1907 (last documented refresher-training return) |
| Définition | Reservists who had definitively left the colours and were recalled for short refresher training: c.80,000 in 1907. They supplement the annual contingent of c.22,500 long-trained men and c.17,000 special reservists. Term of service: 8 years in the active army and its reserves, then 8 years in the second line. A national militia system with very rare exemptions, somewhat on the Swiss model. |

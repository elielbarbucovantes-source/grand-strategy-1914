| Lien | https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/1911_Encyclop%C3%A6dia_Britannica/Servia |
| Fiabilité | faible |
| Calcul | EB1911 war strength 225,000 including landsturm minus 35,000 peace strength = about 190,000 trained reservists on the 1911 establishment. The higher value of 250,000 adopted here allows for the much larger number of men given combat training in the Balkan Wars of 1912-13; the August 1914 mobilisation of 420,597 implies a very large trained or semi-trained manpower pool but also included untrained recruits. This is a rough estimate only. |

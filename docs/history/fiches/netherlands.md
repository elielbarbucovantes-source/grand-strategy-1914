### B. Réserves mobilisables

| Champ | Contenu |
|---|---|
| Valeur retenue | **175 000** |
| Date de référence | 1913-14 reserve organisation; trained reservists available on mobilisation in January 1914 |
| Définition | ESTIMATED trained reservists: under universal conscription with short service with the colours and a multi-year reserve (and Landsturm) liability, the Netherlands could field roughly 200,000 men in August 1914. Subtracting the c.25,000 active peace establishment gives on the order of 175,000 trained reservists. These men were NOT under arms in January 1914. A portion of the mobilised total was only partly trained Landsturm, so the strictly 'trained' figure may be lower. |
| Source | `SEC_west_SYB1914` — Statesman's Year-Book, 1914 edition (ed. J. Scott Keltie), national entries for Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg and Switzerland, sections 'Defence' / 'Army'. The annual compilation of official government returns closest in date to January 1914; the standard reference for peace establishments of the European armies. Used here for order-of-magnitude and structure; exact figures could not be re-verified in this environment (see METHODOLOGY). |
| Page / passage | Netherlands, 'Defence — Army' (reserve and Landsturm provisions) |
| Lien | — |
| Fiabilité | moyenne |
| Calcul | ESTIMATE by subtraction: c.200,000 mobilised (August 1914, standard literature) minus c.25,000 active peace establishment (above) = c.175,000. Both inputs are approximate, so the result is flagged as an estimate and must not be read as a verified return. |
